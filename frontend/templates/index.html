Upload your resume in PDF or DOCX format. Our NLP engine extracts your candidate details, categorizes 100+ technical skills, calculates an application-generated Resume Score (0-100), and matches you with top suitable job roles using TF-IDF Vectorization and Cosine Similarity.

System Architecture & Features

Designed for B.Tech Final Year Viva Demonstration

1. NLP Resume Parsing

Extracts structured contact info, education history, projects, certifications, and technical skills across 8 core domains using regular expressions and SpaCy text processing.

2. Resume Scorer (0-100)

Calculates a multi-factor score breakdown based on Skills (30%), Experience (20%), Projects (20%), Education (15%), Certifications (10%), and Profile Completeness (5%).

3. TF-IDF & Skill Gap Engine

Vectorizes text and calculates Cosine Similarity + Skill Overlap against 30+ realistic job listings. Provides customized learning recommendations for missing skills.

How The Recommendation Engine Works

Step-by-step workflow from upload to job match ranking

1

Resume Upload

Drag & drop PDF or DOCX file. System validates extension and file integrity.

2

NLP Extraction

Normalizes text and categorizes 100+ technical skills into Programming, AI, Web, and Cloud.

3

Matching Algorithm

Computes 45% TF-IDF Similarity + 35% Skill Match + 10% Experience + 10% Education.

4

Skill Gap Recommendations

Displays top recommended jobs, matching vs missing skills, and learning topics.
